Published by Hassan Alvi | Category: Time Zones

Have you ever wondered why Dubai is 1 hour behind Karachi, or why London is 5 hours behind Pakistan? Time zones are created based on the Earth's rotation around the Sun. The planet is divided into 24 time zones, each representing one hour of the day.

2. Karachi to Dubai & New York Time Difference Explained

Published by Hassan Alvi | Category: Guides

Managing international client calls or flying across continents requires knowing precise local times. Here is a quick time zone difference guide for key major cities:

  • Karachi (PKT - UTC+5): 1 hour ahead of Dubai, 5 hours ahead of GMT/London (Winter).
  • Dubai (GST - UTC+4): 1 hour behind Pakistan standard time.
  • New York (EST - UTC-5): 10 hours behind Karachi during Standard Time.

3. Why DST (Daylight Saving Time) Changes Global Time Schedules

Published by Hassan Alvi | Category: World Clock Insights

Daylight Saving Time (DST) is the practice of advancing clocks during warmer months so that darkness falls at a later clock time. Countries in Europe and North America shift their clocks forward by 1 hour in spring and back in autumn.

Countries like Pakistan, UAE, and Saudi Arabia do not observe DST. This is why the time difference between Asian cities and US/UK cities changes twice a year. Our real-time World Clock automatically updates these DST changes for 30+ cities globally.
