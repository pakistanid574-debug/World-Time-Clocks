❓ Norway Time & Travel FAQs

What time zone is Norway in?
Norway uses Central European Time (CET) in winter and Central European Summer Time (CEST) during daylight saving time.
What are famous places to visit in Norway?
Popular destinations include Geirangerfjord, Preikestolen, Trolltunga, Lofoten Islands, Tromsø, Bergen, Flåm and Oslo attractions.
What is Norway known for?
Norway is widely known for its fjords, Arctic landscapes, scenic mountains, historic cities and northern lights experiences.
