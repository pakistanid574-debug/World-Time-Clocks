Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is the time difference between Pakistan and India?

India (IST - UTC+5:30) is 30 minutes ahead of Pakistan (PKT - UTC+5:00).

What time zone is Manchester, UK?

Manchester uses GMT (UTC+0) in winter and BST (UTC+1) during summer daylight savings.

What time zone is Florida, USA?

Florida predominantly falls under Eastern Standard Time (EST - UTC-5 / EDT - UTC-4).
