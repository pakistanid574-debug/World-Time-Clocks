Happy New Year Images & How Global Time Zones Celebrate First

Published by Hassan Alvi | Category: Celebrations & Media

As midnight approaches across the globe, millions of people search for the best Happy New Year images, greetings, and wallpapers to share with family, friends, and remote teammates. But did you know that New Year celebrations travel across 24 different time zones over a full 26-hour period?

Which Country Celebrates New Year First?

Due to the Earth's rotation and the placement of the International Date Line, the Pacific island nation of Kiribati (Kiritimati/Christmas Island) is the very first place to welcome the New Year at UTC+14. Shortly after, Auckland, New Zealand, and Sydney, Australia light up their skies with world-famous firework displays.

How the New Year Travels Across Major World Cities

  • Kiribati & Samoa: First to enter the New Year (UTC+14)
  • Auckland, New Zealand: 2 hours later (UTC+13)
  • Sydney, Australia: 4 hours later (UTC+11)
  • Tokyo, Japan: 6 hours later (UTC+9)
  • Karachi & Islamabad, Pakistan: 10 hours later (UTC+5)
  • Dubai, UAE: 11 hours later (UTC+4)
  • London, UK: 15 hours later (UTC+0)
  • New York, USA: 20 hours later (UTC-5)
