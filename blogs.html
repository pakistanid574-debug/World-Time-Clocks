How Time Zones Work: A Complete Guide to World Time Difference

Published by Hassan Alvi | Category: Time Zones

Have you ever wondered why Dubai is 1 hour behind Karachi, or why London is 5 hours behind Pakistan? Time zones are created based on the Earth's rotation around the Sun. The planet is divided into 24 time zones, each representing one hour of the day. Coordinated Universal Time (UTC) serves as the primary time standard by which the world regulates clocks and time.

What is Daylight Saving Time (DST) and Which Countries Use It?

Published by Hassan Alvi | Category: Global Time Standards

Daylight Saving Time (DST) is the practice of advancing clocks during warmer months so that darkness falls at a later clock hour. Countries in North America and Europe actively switch to DST, resulting in seasonal time changes twice a year. Understanding DST helps remote workers and global businesses avoid missing important meetings.
